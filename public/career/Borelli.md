## Abstract



Research engineer working on 3D reconstruction from satellite imagery.

Developed methods based on 3D Gaussian Splatting for Earth observation, with a focus on handling inaccurate camera parameters and integrating multispectral satellite data. This work led to a [publication](https://isprs-annals.copernicus.org/articles/XI-2-2026/217/2026/) accepted at **ISPRS 2026**.
